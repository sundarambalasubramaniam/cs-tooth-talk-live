Programme Formats

Six Ways to Learn and Connect

Every format is crafted to fit around your schedule and deliver genuine value — from quick lunch talks to full-day intensive workshops.

Networking Evenings

Relaxed, informal evenings where dentists, hygienists, and specialists share experiences over food and drink. No slides required.

Clinical Workshops

Hands-on, small-group sessions covering emerging techniques — composite artistry, minimal-prep veneers, and digital workflow integration.

Speaker Series

Monthly evening talks from leading clinicians and researchers sharing the evidence behind their practice — always followed by open Q&A.

Mentorship Programme

Structured 6-month pairings matching newly qualified dentists with experienced principals and specialists for career and clinical guidance.

Online Community

A private forum and monthly virtual clinic where members share cases, ask questions, and stay connected between in-person events.

Annual Conference

Our flagship full-day event with 500+ attendees, 12 keynote sessions, live demos, an exhibition area, and the Tooth Talk Community Awards.
